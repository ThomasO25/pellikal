# SEO-SEARCH-RESULT.md

The homepage's search-result presentation, and what Google actually does with
it. **22 September 2026.**

## What was changed and why

The organic Google result for `https://www.pellikal.com/` was positioned
around *"Premium Window Film for Hamptons & East End Homes"*, and the visible
top of the page led with the Hamptons, the East End and LLumar. Paid traffic
was recently broadened to all of Long Island and NYC, so the first impression
was narrower than the business.

Homepage now (old → new):

| Element | Old | New |
|---|---|---|
| `<title>` | Premium Window Film for Hamptons & East End Homes \| Pellikal | Window Film Installation on Long Island & NYC \| Pellikal |
| meta description | Premium residential window film for the Hamptons, East End and Long Island… | Residential and commercial window film installation across Nassau, Suffolk, Queens, Brooklyn and Manhattan. Reduce heat, glare and UV. Get a free quote. |
| `og:title` / `twitter:title` | (old title) | (new title) |
| `og:description` | (old description) | (new description) |
| Eyebrow | The Hamptons · East End · North Fork · Long Island | Long Island · Nassau · Suffolk · Queens · Brooklyn · Manhattan |
| H1 | Protect What You've Built. | Professional Window Film for Long Island & NYC |
| Hero paragraph | Premium LLumar® window film for East End, Hamptons and Long Island homes… | Residential and commercial window film, professionally installed to help reduce heat, glare and UV while improving comfort and privacy. Free, no-pressure quotes across Long Island and New York City. |

Unchanged on purpose: canonical, `og:url`, `og:image`, favicons, manifest,
the JSON-LD (already described the business as residential + commercial
across Long Island and NYC, with `areaServed` matching `site.config.json`),
the LLumar SelectPro trust strip and section directly under the hero, and
every Hamptons / East End / North Fork / coastal section deeper on the page.
That market is still served and still described; it simply no longer *is*
the homepage.

## The CMS caveat — read this before deploying

The H1 and hero paragraph are CMS-editable (`data-content="hero_headline"`
and `"hero_subtitle"`). `js/main.js` replaces their text at runtime with the
published `site_content` rows, and migration `0002` seeded those rows with
the **old** copy. So on the live site, the database would put *"Protect What
You've Built."* back on the page for every JavaScript-enabled visitor — and
for Google's renderer — regardless of the new HTML.

Two ways to fix it; do one:

1. Run `supabase/migrations/0003_homepage_hero_repositioning.sql` on the live
   project. It is guarded: it only updates rows that still hold the `0002`
   seed text, so a headline the owner has edited in `/admin/` is untouched.
2. Or open `/admin/` → Home page text → set the headline and subtitle to the
   new copy → Publish.

Verify after deploying: load the homepage with JavaScript on and confirm the
H1 reads *Professional Window Film for Long Island & NYC*.

## What Google will do with it

- `<title>` and the meta description express the **preferred** presentation.
  Google may still rewrite the title link or the snippet for a given query,
  drawing on the H1, prominent page text, anchor text and other signals —
  which is why the visible hero was changed and not just the metadata.
- Google has to recrawl and reprocess the page before the result changes.
  That can take days to weeks. No exact timing is promised.
- After deployment, in **Google Search Console**: URL Inspection →
  `https://www.pellikal.com/` → *Test live URL* → **Request Indexing**.
- `sitemap.xml` (generated by `tools/build.py`) should remain submitted in
  Search Console. It lists only indexable public pages; `/thankyou/` and
  `/admin/` are excluded.

## Page-level metadata stays distinct

| Page | Title |
|---|---|
| `/` | Window Film Installation on Long Island & NYC |
| `/residential/` | Residential Window Film in Long Island & NYC |
| `/commercial/` | Commercial Window Film in Long Island & NYC |
| `/window-inserts/` | Window Inserts for Noise Reduction & Comfort |

Each page targets its own intent. Do not converge them on one query.

## Not an explanation for the paid-traffic problem

This tidies the organic listing. It does not account for paid clicks that do
not convert; the Search Terms report, Presence-only location targeting and the
actual ad final URLs are the next things to inspect in Google Ads.
