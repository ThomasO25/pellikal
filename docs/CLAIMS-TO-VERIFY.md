## 3. Warranty wording — changed sitewide

**Old (removed everywhere):**
> "Manufacturer-backed lifetime warranty available"

**New:**
> "Manufacturer-backed warranty available on qualifying products"

plus, on the homepage SelectPro section:
> "Warranty terms are set and honoured by the film manufacturer. Coverage varies
> by product and application and is not automatic."

This removes any implication that every film qualifies, that coverage is
automatic, that every installation is covered for life, or that Pellikal sets
manufacturer terms.

**⚠️ OWNER APPROVAL REQUIRED** on the final warranty sentence, and on how the
paid LLumar warranty-card option should be described once pricing is decided.

## 4. Deliberately not claimed

The site does **not** say, and must not be edited to say:

- Any award, ranking, "#1", "preferred installer" or "best in the East End"
- Any third-party endorsement or accreditation beyond being a Llumar dealer
- Any guarantee that film prevents *all* fading — the homepage explicitly states
  it cannot, since fading is also driven by visible light, heat and time
- Any compliance guarantee for any regulation
- Any customer testimonial that did not come from a real customer
- Any physical office or address on the East End
