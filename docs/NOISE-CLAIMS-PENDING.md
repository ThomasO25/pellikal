# NOISE-CLAIMS-PENDING.md

Manufacturer performance figures for `/window-inserts/` — **LIVE since 15
September 2026**, enabled at the owner's request for "a real decibel figure".
This file is now the record of what the switch does and how to revert it.

## Current state

The three cards in the "Quieter rooms" section are generated by
`tools/build.py` → `insert_claims()` from one switch:

```json
"windowInserts": { "manufacturerClaims": { "enabled": true, ... } }   // site.config.json
```

| `enabled` | What renders |
|---|---|
| `false` | Qualitative cards — "Quieter", "More comfortable", "Made for your window". Names no manufacturer, carries no figures. |
| `true` (now) | **Up to 70% noise reduction** · **Average 20% energy savings** · **Over 99% perfect fit rate**, each sentence attributed ("Indow Acoustic Grade inserts are designed to…", "Indow reports…"), "with its Snug Fit compression tubing", plus a note that the figures are Indow's and not a guarantee. Noise first, energy second. Never "our patented". |

The enabled variant was rendered and inspected on 14 Sep (wording, attribution,
no "our patented", no soundproof/silent language) and then switched back off.

## Revert — one line, no developer needed

Set `"enabled": false` in `site.config.json`, run `python3 tools/build.py`,
and the page goes back to qualitative cards with no manufacturer named and no
band. Do that immediately if the product sold is not Indow Acoustic Grade.

## Why it is not simply on

The website has never identified the inserts as Indow. "Indow reports…" is a
statement that Pellikal sells Indow. That is very likely true from the
material the owner has sent, but "very likely" is not a basis for a published
manufacturer claim. One sentence from the owner closes it.

## The tested-dBA band — added then removed the same day

A compact band with the 18.9 dBA / 10–12 dBA figures sat above "How it works"
for part of 15 Sep. The owner then decided the page should stay general on
noise ("helps reduce outside noise", "quieter rooms") rather than lead with
exact decibels, so the band, its generator (`insert_db()`) and its CSS were
removed. No exact dB figure appears anywhere on the page. The three
manufacturer-attributed cards (70% / 20% / 99%) remain, still under the same
switch.

## Optional, not wired

The FAQ answer "How much can window inserts reduce outside noise?" can gain,
after its first sentence:

> In independent testing published by Indow, Acoustic Grade inserts reduced
> sound through operable single-pane windows by up to 18.9 dBA (about 70%)
> and through operable double-pane windows by roughly 10–12 dBA (about 50%),
> under tested conditions.

Never convert dBA into "X times quieter". Keep single- and double-pane
figures separate. Avoid Indow's own "nearly soundproof" phrasing.
