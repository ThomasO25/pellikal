## 3. Deliberately NOT claimed

To stay inside what can be supported, the site does **not** assert any of the
following, and this wording must not be added without documented backing:

- Certification standards, training requirements or qualification criteria for
  the SelectPro programme
- That SelectPro dealers are vetted, screened, ranked or preferred
- Any award, endorsement or recommendation by LLumar
- Any exclusivity ("only SelectPro dealer on the East End" etc.)
- Any installation guarantee offered by LLumar
- Specific product performance figures attributed to LLumar

The section explains the practical benefit to the customer instead of asserting
programme rules that have not been verified.

---

## 4. Performance figures

No LLumar-specific performance number (VLT, UV rejection, TSER, heat rejection)
is published anywhere except the generic "up to 99% of UV" claim documented in
`CLAIMS-TO-VERIFY.md` §2.1.

If product specifications are added later, they must be transcribed from LLumar
technical data sheets for the exact films offered — never estimated, rounded up
or carried over from another product.
