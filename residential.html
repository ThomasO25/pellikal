Why homeowners choose film

Five upgrades from one thin, invisible layer

Quality residential film works quietly in the background — you'll feel the difference long before you notice the glass.

Year-round comfort

Even out hot and cold spots so the rooms you avoid in summer become the ones you actually want to use.

Daytime privacy

Reflective and frosted films keep passersby from seeing in during the day while you keep your view and natural light.

UV protection

Blocks up to 99% of UV rays that fade hardwood floors, furniture, artwork and fabrics.

Lower energy bills

By rejecting solar heat gain, film eases the load on your AC in summer — and Low-E options help hold heat in winter.

Reduced glare

Cut harsh glare on TVs, laptops and monitors so you can work and relax without fighting the afternoon sun.

Added safety

Security films help hold shattered glass together on impact — protection against storms, accidents and break-ins.

Room by room

Where window film makes the biggest difference

Every home has a few problem windows. These are the spaces homeowners ask us about most.

  • Living rooms — tame glare on the TV and keep the space comfortable all afternoon
  • Bathrooms — frosted privacy film that still lets daylight through
  • Bedrooms — cut heat and morning glare for better sleep
  • Sliding & patio doors — reduce hot spots and fading near large glass
  • Sunrooms & additions — make all-glass spaces usable in peak summer sun
