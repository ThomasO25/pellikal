Interior protection

Help protect the interiors you've invested in.

Sunlight can contribute to fading and deterioration over time. Properly selected window film can significantly reduce UV exposure while helping manage solar heat and glare — without closing the room off from daylight.

UV is one contributor to fading. Visible light, heat and time also play a part, so film reduces exposure rather than eliminating fading.

Fine art

Paintings, prints and photography are among the most light-sensitive things in a home.

Hardwood & stone

Wide-plank and stained floors bleach unevenly where direct sun tracks across them.

Rugs & textiles

Natural dyes and fibres in rugs, drapery and upholstery are especially vulnerable.

Custom furniture

Leather, veneers and bespoke finishes hold their colour longer out of direct UV.
