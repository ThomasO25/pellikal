The problem with bare glass

Your windows are quietly costing you comfort and money

Untreated glass lets in heat you pay to remove, glare that washes out screens, and UV that fades floors and furniture. Window film fixes the glass you already have — a fraction of the cost and disruption of replacement.

Heat & hot spots

Solar heat gain overworks your HVAC and creates rooms nobody wants to sit in. Film rejects a large share of that heat before it enters.

Glare & UV fading

Harsh glare strains eyes and hides screens; UV bleaches wood, fabric and art. Quality film blocks up to 99% of UV and tames glare.

Privacy & safety

Clear glass exposes interiors and shatters on impact. Privacy and security films add daytime privacy and hold broken glass together.

By the numbers

How much energy is escaping your windows?

Energy loss
30%

Windows can account for up to 30% of a building's heating and cooling energy loss — a major, fixable source of waste.

Single-pane heat loss
10×

Single-pane glass can lose up to 10× more heat than an insulated wall of the same area. Older units lose efficiency over time.

Efficiency gain
80%

Dual-pane windows with solar film or similar coatings can reduce energy loss by up to 80%, depending on heat-gain reduction.
