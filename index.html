Interior protection

Protect What Matters Inside.

Ultraviolet light and prolonged solar exposure contribute to fading and deterioration of interior finishes. Quality window film significantly reduces the UV reaching your rooms — it cannot prevent all fading, which is also driven by visible light, heat and time, but it removes the largest single contributor.

Fine art

Paintings, prints and photography are among the most light-sensitive things in a home.

Hardwood flooring

Wide-plank and stained floors bleach unevenly where direct sun tracks across them.

Rugs & textiles

Natural dyes and fibres in rugs, drapery and upholstery are especially vulnerable.

Designer furniture

Leather, veneers and custom finishes hold their colour far longer out of direct UV.
