Film vs. replacement

Why window film instead of new windows?

For heat, glare, UV and privacy, film solves the problem for a fraction of the cost and disruption — often in a single day, on the glass you already have.

Window film

  • A fraction of the cost of replacement
  • Usually installed in a day — family or tenants stay in place
  • Cuts heat and glare, blocks up to 99% of UV, adds privacy & safety
  • Keeps your existing windows and frames

Full replacement

  • Best when glass is failing, foggy, or seals are broken
  • Higher cost and a longer, more disruptive project
  • The right call for rot, drafts or structural window issues
  • We’ll tell you honestly when replacement beats film
