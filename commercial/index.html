The business case for film

A fast, low-disruption building upgrade

Compared with mechanical retrofits or glass replacement, film is quick to install and easy to budget — with benefits tenants notice immediately.

Energy efficiency

Solar and Low-E films cut heat gain and loss, easing HVAC load and reducing cooling costs across your portfolio.

Tenant comfort

Even out hot perimeter offices and sunny conference rooms — fewer complaints and better retention.

Privacy & branding

Add privacy to conference rooms and ground floors, or a clean, consistent exterior look across a facade.

Security & safety

Safety film helps hold glass together against impact, storms, break-ins and accidental breakage.

Glare control

Reduce screen glare in offices and reception areas for a more productive working environment.

Anti-graffiti protection

Shield storefront glass and elevators — swap the film instead of costly glass after vandalism.
