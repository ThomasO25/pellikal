Accessibility

Accessibility Statement

We want this website to be usable by everyone, including people using assistive technology.

Last updated: August 2026

Our approach

We aim to follow the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA as a practical standard. This is a statement of intent and ongoing effort, not a claim of certification or of full conformance.

What we have built in

  • Text alternatives on images that carry meaning
  • A skip link to jump straight to the main content
  • Keyboard access for the menu, accordions, gallery and forms
  • Visible focus outlines so you can see where you are
  • Form fields with real, associated labels
  • Colour combinations chosen for contrast against their backgrounds
  • Headings in a logical order, and landmark regions for screen readers
  • Reduced or removed motion when your device requests it
  • Layouts that reflow for zoom and small screens

Known limitations

Some areas may not yet be fully accessible. Photographs added by our team may occasionally lack a full description until we update them, and third-party components such as embedded forms are partly outside our control. We fix issues as we find them.

Tell us about a problem

If any part of this site is difficult to use, please tell us and we will help directly and work on a fix. Call or text 516-336-9586, or email info@pellikal.com. Please mention the page and what happened. We aim to respond within a few business days.

Getting the information another way

If you cannot access something here, we will provide the same information by phone, text or email, including pricing and product details.
