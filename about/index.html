About Pellikal

Careful work on other people's finished homes

Pellikal installs premium window film across Long Island, the East End and New York City — most often in homes where the interiors, the art and the views all matter.

How we approach the work

Most of our work happens in homes that are already finished and lived in. That shapes everything about how we operate: what we recommend, how we protect the space while we're in it, and what we tell you when film isn't the right answer.

Honest recommendations

We'd rather lose a job than sell the wrong film. If a darker film would fix your glare but ruin the view you built the room around, we'll say so. If your glass is failing and replacement makes more sense than film, we'll tell you that too. The recommendation you get is the one we'd make in our own house.

Premium product selection

We install LLumar® window film and are part of the LLumar SelectPro™ dealer network. Film is chosen for your specific glass, orientation and goals rather than pulled off a single default roll — a west-facing wall of glass over water is a different problem from a north-facing bathroom. Manufacturer-backed warranty coverage is available on qualifying products; terms are set by the manufacturer and vary by product and application.

Respect for your property

We protect floors and furnishings before we start, work cleanly, and leave the space the way we found it — minus the glare. Installations are carried out by experienced Pellikal professionals and approved installation teams, so the people in your home are people we're accountable for.

Careful workmanship

Film is unforgiving: edges, dust and alignment are visible forever, at eye level, in daylight. We take the time to prepare the glass properly and we walk the finished work with you before we leave. If something isn't right, we want to hear it while we're still there.

Where we work

Residential work across the Hamptons, the East End, the North Fork, the North Shore and Long Island's coastal communities, plus luxury residences in Manhattan. We also take on commercial projects — offices, retail and multifamily buildings. Pellikal is a service-area business: we travel to you and do not operate a public storefront.
