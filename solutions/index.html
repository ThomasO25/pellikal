Every film type, explained

Window Film Solutions for Homes & Buildings

Different problems call for different films. Here's a straight-talk guide to what each type does and what it's best for — so you can walk into your consultation already knowing the options.

Low-EPrivacySolar & Spectrally SelectiveNeutralCeramicSecurityAnti-Graffiti

01 · Insulation

Low-E Window Film

A low-emissivity film that improves the insulating performance of existing glass — reducing heat transfer in both directions.

Best for: year-round comfort and energy savings in homes and buildings with older or single-pane glass.

  • Keeps summer heat out and winter warmth in
  • Eases HVAC load across the seasons
  • Improves comfort near large or aging windows

02 · Privacy

Dual-Reflective & Privacy Film

Films that limit the view in while preserving your view out and your natural light. Frosted films add all-day, opaque privacy.

Best for: ground-floor rooms, street-facing windows, bathrooms and conference rooms.

  • Daytime privacy with dual-reflective film
  • Round-the-clock privacy with frosted / decorative film
  • Clean, consistent exterior appearance

Note: standard reflective privacy film works by daylight; at night with interior lights on the effect reverses. We'll recommend frosted or dual-reflective film if you need nighttime privacy.

03 · Heat & glare

Solar & Spectrally Selective Film

Solar control film rejects a large share of the sun's heat and glare while letting daylight through. Spectrally selective films block more infrared heat while staying highly transparent — heat rejection without a dark look.

Best for: sun-drenched and west-facing rooms, sunrooms and glass facades.

  • Rejects solar heat gain to lower cooling costs
  • Cuts glare on screens and work surfaces
  • Spectrally selective options keep glass clear and bright
  • Blocks up to 99% of UV rays

04 · Natural look

Neutral Film

A low-reflectivity film that delivers heat and UV benefits while keeping the glass looking close to its natural, untinted appearance — no strong color, no mirror effect.

Best for: anyone who wants performance without changing the look, and locations with appearance or HOA guidelines.

  • Subtle, natural appearance inside and out
  • Solar and UV protection without heavy tint
  • Great where a reflective look isn't wanted

05 · Premium performance

Ceramic Window Film

A premium, non-metal film built around ceramic particles. Excellent heat rejection and clarity with a neutral finish — and no interference with cellular, Wi-Fi or other signals.

Best for: top-tier heat rejection and optical clarity where signal performance and looks both matter.

  • Excellent infrared heat rejection
  • High clarity with low reflectivity
  • No signal interference — metal-free
  • Blocks up to 99% of UV rays

06 · Protection

Security & Safety Film

A thick, tear-resistant film — often paired with a structural sealant — that helps hold glass together when it breaks, slowing forced entry and containing shards.

Best for: ground-floor and storefront glass, doors, schools and healthcare.

  • Helps hold shattered glass in place on impact
  • Slows smash-and-grab and forced-entry attempts
  • Adds protection against storms and accidental breakage

Note: no film makes glass unbreakable. Security film makes glass harder and slower to breach and reduces injury from flying glass.

07 · Surface protection

Anti-Graffiti Film

A clear, sacrificial layer applied over glass, mirrors and metal. If it's scratched, etched or tagged, you remove and replace the film — not the expensive surface underneath.

Best for: storefronts, glass doors, elevators, restrooms and transit.

  • Protects glass from etching, scratching and paint
  • Fast, low-cost recovery — swap the film, keep the glass
  • Nearly invisible once installed
