Every film type, explained

Window Film Solutions for Homes & Buildings

Different problems call for different films. Here's a straight-talk guide to what each type does and what it's best for — so you can walk into your consultation already knowing the options.

Low-E Privacy / Dual-Reflective Solar & Spectrally Selective Neutral Ceramic Security & Safety Anti-Graffiti

01 · Insulation

Low-E Window Film

A low-emissivity film that improves the insulating performance of existing glass — reducing heat transfer in both directions.

Best for: year-round comfort and energy savings in homes and buildings with older or single-pane glass, and any space that's too hot in summer and too cold in winter.

  • Keeps summer heat out and winter warmth in
  • Eases HVAC load across the seasons
  • Improves comfort near large or aging windows

Ask about Low-E film

02 · Privacy

Dual-Reflective & Privacy Film

Films that limit the view in while preserving your view out and your natural light. Dual-reflective options keep a lower-glare interior look; frosted films add all-day, opaque privacy.

Best for: ground-floor rooms, street-facing windows, bathrooms, conference rooms and any space where you want privacy without losing daylight.

  • Daytime privacy with dual-reflective film
  • Round-the-clock privacy with frosted / decorative film
  • Clean, consistent exterior appearance

Note: standard reflective privacy film works by daylight. At night, with interior lights on, the effect reverses — we'll recommend frosted or dual-reflective film if you need nighttime privacy.

Ask about privacy film

03 · Heat & glare

Solar & Spectrally Selective Film

Solar control film rejects a large share of the sun's heat and glare while letting daylight through. Spectrally selective films go a step further — blocking more infrared heat while staying highly transparent, so you get heat rejection without a dark, tinted look.

Best for: sun-drenched and west-facing rooms, sunrooms, glass facades, and anyone who wants cooler spaces and less glare without heavily darkening the glass.

  • Rejects solar heat gain to lower cooling costs
  • Cuts glare on screens and work surfaces
  • Spectrally selective options keep glass clear and bright
  • Blocks up to 99% of UV rays

Ask about solar film

04 · Natural look

Neutral Film

A low-key, low-reflectivity film that delivers heat and UV benefits while keeping the glass looking close to its natural, untinted appearance — no strong color, no mirror effect.

Best for: homeowners and buildings that want performance without changing the look of the windows, and locations with appearance guidelines or HOA restrictions.

  • Subtle, natural appearance inside and out
  • Solar and UV protection without heavy tint
  • Great where a reflective look isn't wanted

Ask about neutral film

05 · Premium performance

Ceramic Window Film

A premium, non-metal film built around ceramic particles. It delivers excellent heat rejection and clarity with a neutral, low-reflective finish — and because it contains no metal, it won't interfere with cellular, Wi-Fi or other signals.

Best for: anyone who wants top-tier heat rejection and optical clarity, and spaces where signal performance and a clean look both matter.

  • Excellent infrared heat rejection
  • High clarity with low reflectivity
  • No signal interference — metal-free construction
  • Blocks up to 99% of UV rays

Ask about ceramic film

06 · Protection

Security & Safety Film

A thick, tear-resistant film — often paired with a structural sealant at the edges — that helps hold glass together when it breaks, slowing forced entry and containing dangerous shards.

Best for: ground-floor and storefront glass, doors, schools, healthcare and any space where you want to make glass harder to break through and safer when it does break.

  • Helps hold shattered glass in place on impact
  • Slows smash-and-grab and forced-entry attempts
  • Adds protection against storms and accidental breakage

Note: no film makes glass unbreakable. Security film is designed to make glass more difficult and time-consuming to breach and to reduce injury from flying glass.

Ask about security film

07 · Surface protection

Anti-Graffiti Film

A clear, sacrificial layer applied over glass, mirrors, metal and other smooth surfaces. If it's scratched, etched or tagged, you simply remove and replace the film — not the expensive surface underneath.

Best for: storefront windows, glass doors, elevators, restrooms, transit and any high-traffic surface exposed to vandalism or wear.

  • Protects glass from etching, scratching and paint
  • Fast, low-cost recovery — swap the film, keep the glass
  • Nearly invisible once installed
